# ORG-POLICY-004 prospective fixed protocol

The scientific design and all formulas in ASSIGNMENT.md are adopted without fitting or tuning. Master seed 2026092106; 48 fresh histories generated by unchanged baseline_model.train and regime-specific train seeds. Freeze scientific source, config, PRETRAIN_ROSTER.json (all target/transfer streams, canonical family strings) and BOOTSTRAP_INDICES.json before training. Target generation matches original semantics: absolute family angle (uniform [0,2pi) for the reference), then random sign; streams are shared across regimes by history/family/trial index. All training and early transfer/checkpoints for the entire cohort finish before any scientific generation-6 outcome. Three policies use only their own checkpoint population and the allowed known quadratic model. Both predictions, O-minus-R predicted loss, choice and tie are saved for M/G/E. No future draws, other-arm state, historical labels or later outcomes enter policy.decisions. Restore saved RNG states to evaluate four absolute-rule continuations; deterministic policy trajectories are exactly the branch chosen prospectively.

For every Gaussian component, K=(I+2 beta T A)^−1, V=KT, log Z=−½logdet(I+2 beta T A)−beta rᵀAKr, q=(Kr)ᵀA(Kr)+tr(AV). Matrix product order is retained. T=0 and singular T are valid. Stable log-sum-exp equal-prior mixtures weight component q by Z. M uses N(mu,0)/N(mu,C); G uses N(mu,S)/N(mu,S+C), S denominator N; E uses all N empirical point parents and N corresponding Gaussian offspring. All candidate policies have the same allowed observations; their declared summaries differ. This is an ideal normalized-weight mixture approximation to the finite sequential sampler, not an exact finite-population forecast or black-box optimizer benchmark.

Lead endpoint: E−M at generation 25, separately by historical regime, equally averaging all eight families, eight trials and both early states within history. Also E−G, G−M and each M/G/E versus CONTINUE, SWITCH and HALF. Retain all policy levels, absolute O/R branches, family × early-arm subgroups and all individual-history estimates. HINDSIGHT is only max(realized O,R) at the endpoint, explicitly nonattainable as a policy and not an unbiased optimal expected policy. HALF is the expected outcome of an independent 50/50 choice, not a blended population.

The prospective usefulness target is .02 normalized performance units, an engineering target, not biological calibration. Each policy contrast is classified relative to both zero and .02 using its interval; no significance search, threshold-driven extra seeds or endpoint revisions. Paired bootstrap: 2,000 resamples of 24 whole histories, seed2026092107, structured then isotropic. The frozen regime-specific index arrays are reused for every metric, family and early-arm table. Linear percentile interpolation at (B−1)q. Pointwise exploratory intervals, dependent subgroup comparisons, no multiplicity-controlled or separate-interval history-regime interaction claim.
