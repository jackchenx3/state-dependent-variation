This experiment tests dependence of the v1 transfer result on guaranteed survival of best candidates; it is not new training replication or a test of probabilistic training.

## Frozen historical inputs and single changed rule

Read the v1 result with SHA256 `5e16b6d027a1a4a330bc36ad132efbed00e32e7fb4d9e48876d225af25a05a84`. Reuse all 48 stored inherited angles and the exact 3,072 target/trial panel entries. Reset transfer phenotypes to zero, keep 32 individuals, Gaussian principal SDs 0.12/0.02, frozen inherited orientation, quarter-turn comparator, and 25 generations. No historical simulation is called. Original historical regime labels are retained only to group the fixed inputs.

For each arm/generation, generate 32 children exactly as in v1. From the 32 parents plus 32 children, sample 32 distinct candidate indices sequentially without replacement. At each draw use weights proportional to exp(-10 * squared target distance) among remaining candidates. Retain the resulting draw order. Equal phenotypes are distinct candidates. Coefficient 10 is a fixed exploratory choice, not a biologically calibrated value. Recenter exponentials by the lowest remaining distance at every draw for stability; this does not change normalized probabilities. Underflow, if any, is handled by recentering after higher-weight candidates are removed, not by permanently removing low-weight candidates.

Master seed 2026092102 is fixed before output. Each paired trial records SHA256-derived integer seeds for separate `v2-mutation` and `v2-survival` random streams keyed by historical index, family string, and trial index. Both arms share Gaussian innovations and survival uniforms for variance reduction; each arm uses its own remaining-candidate weights. Regimes share test streams for the same historical index/family/trial as v1 did. These streams are fresh relative to v1. No seed, coefficient, horizon or cohort adjustment after outcomes.

Predictor remains exactly the v1 function and values: directional variance advantage `(major_sd^2-minor_sd^2)*cos(2*(inherited angle-target angle))`. Baseline_model.py is an unchanged copy of v1's reviewed model used for the predictor, basic geometry and summary functions; its training/elitist transfer functions are never called.

## Endpoints, correctness and complete analysis

Endpoint remains `1 - population mean squared target distance / initial squared distance` at generation 25. Positive/negative *between-arm delta* means organized better/worse than rotated. Probabilistic survival permits generation-level declines and arbitrarily negative normalized improvement. Scores must be finite and no greater than 1 (1e-12 roundoff allowance); do NOT impose a nonnegative lower bound or monotonicity. Report absolute arm scores, declines and negative outcomes as findings.

Tests precede science on the allocated node: exact first/conditional categorical CDF oracles, no replacement with identical phenotypes, possible death of best candidate, numerical recentering, invalid inputs, negative scores, joint rotation equivariance, deterministic disjoint streams, no training/outcome leakage, target/angle reuse, frozen coefficient, full-panel invariants, negative-tolerant validators and inference. Any failed test stops the job before science. No automatic scientific retry.

Retain every history, target, seed, both endpoints, paired delta and all 25 trajectory pairs. Apply v1's unchanged equal-history averaging, pointwise 95% 2,000-resample history bootstrap, sign accuracy/both naive baselines, 1e-12 predictor/crossing tie rule and positive-to-negative localization categories. No multiplicity-adjusted, practical-equivalence or confirmatory inference. Inferential units remain 24 inherited history inputs per regime, paired to v1; this is not a fresh set of training histories. Accuracy over seven angles has correlated observations, not 168 independent units.

Compare v2 and v1 at every family/regime, including mean performance of both arms, between-arm delta with the original pointwise intervals, mean per-history v2-minus-v1 delta, predictor accuracy, all bracket records, and every disagreement. For paired changes use the same history index across versions and a separate fixed bootstrap namespace `v2-v1-paired` with 2,000 resamples. These exploratory conditional comparisons also contain fresh transfer Monte Carlo noise; the stored v1 is not rerun with v2 seeds. Report per-arm endpoint-negative fraction, per-arm fraction with any generation decrease, total declining transitions, and per-arm >.99 ceiling fraction, with no exclusions.

The reflection/bisector and quarter-turn symmetries discussed in the external review remain present because probabilistic weights depend only on squared distance. Persistence of good prediction would test robustness to this selection change, not remove the strong geometric constraint or prove uniqueness of crossing/general biological applicability. Predicting a known target geometry is not forecasting an unknown environment.
